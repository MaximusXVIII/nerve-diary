This is a zip of the original DICOM (.dcm) files, organized into a folder per series. Most operating systems won't open .dcm files directly, so you'll likely need a DICOM viewer to inspect them — e.g. MicroDicom on Windows, or Horos on Mac. Metadata (name, DOB, referring clinician, etc.) has been stripped from these copies.
